 About Us
From Bharat to the World

SAG Defence and Aerospace is an Indian technology company developing indigenous defence and aerospace systems. We bring together aerospace engineering, hands-on UAV development and operational discipline to build products that address real mission needs.

Our portfolio spans unmanned aerial platforms, intelligence and surveillance systems, and counter-drone technologies. Our flagship development programme, *Project Yamraj*, focuses on an indigenous supersonic loitering munition, reflecting our ambition to advance Bharat’s defence capabilities.

Led by founders with experience in UAV engineering, flight testing and industrial entrepreneurship, we combine innovation with disciplined execution. We are committed to building reliable products, earning the confidence of those who use them and strengthening India’s technological self-reliance.

Our vision is to create world-class products from Bharat for the world.
